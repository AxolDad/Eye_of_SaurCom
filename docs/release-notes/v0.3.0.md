The first release of **Eye of SaurCom**, the fork of [OnAir](https://github.com/adswill/OnAir) 0.2.9. It keeps every OnAir decoder (145 modes now) and adds what the fork was built for: transmitting, trunking, streaming, ATAK and mesh messaging.

## Downloads in this release

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 15+, Apple silicon | `SaurCom-0.3.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15+, Intel (AVX2) | `SaurCom-0.3.0-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Linux x86-64 / arm64 | `saurcom_0.3.0_amd64.deb`, `saurcom_0.3.0_arm64.deb`, `saurcom-0.3.0-*-portable.tar.gz` |
| Docker | `ghcr.io/axoldad/saurcom:0.3.0` |

**Windows and Android are not in this release yet.** The Windows installer needs its dependency sources gathered for this repository, and there is no Android build job yet. Both follow in a later release. Until then, Windows users can [build from source](https://github.com/AxolDad/Eye_of_SaurCom#build-from-source).

The apps are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. `SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums of every file.

## New since OnAir 0.2.9

**Talking back**
- **Transmit, unlocked.** A one-time agreement to transmit only within your own legal authority turns it on; after that there are no band, power or mode locks. **TX ON AIR** shows in every view while you transmit.
- **Mesh send.** Meshtastic and MeshCore text and position out through the SDR.
- **ATAK / TAK bridge.** Aircraft, ships, APRS and radiosondes go out as Cursor on Target, and TAK contacts show on the map (Tools > Data feeds).

**Listening wider**
- **Trunking and scanning:** P25, DMR Tier III and NXDN Type C call following, trunked systems scanned together with conventional channels, talkgroup names, call recording.
- **Synchronous AM and a co-channel canceller** in the General receiver.
- **NFC / RFID sniffer** at 13.56 MHz.
- **Inmarsat-C:** EGC areas, the land-earth-station list, announcements and messages to ships.
- **WiFi sensing:** presence, motion, breathing and heart rate from RuView's ESP32 CSI frames.

**Aviation**
- **NOTAMs and TFRs** from the FAA NOTAM API, tfr.faa.gov and FIS-B, each falling back to the next; TFRs on the ADS-B map.
- **Flight history:** flights cut into phases, tracks, per-aircraft dossiers and pattern questions, in a local database.
- **ADS-B networks both ways:** feed presets, and asking a service what is in the air.
- **Better transcripts:** cleaned-up audio, and the speech engine is told the call signs, runways and talkgroups on the air.

**Out to the world**
- **Audio out and Icecast:** any output device or an Icecast server.
- **Internet radio** (radio-browser.info) through the same sound output.
- **Video out:** analog TV and FPV pictures as MPEG-2 over UDP.

**Tools and radios**
- **Radio tools:** Wi-Fi deauth flood and evil-twin alarms, Bluetooth skimmer names, a frequency analyzer, and a Wi-Fi / Bluetooth drive log to WiGLE CSV or KML. Receive only.
- **Deep Space Network:** what NASA's dishes are talking to now.
- **Aaronia Spectran V6** through RTSA-Suite, **ESP32 boards** as radios, and **Flipper Zero `.sub`** files in and out.

**Changed**
- New name, new burning-eye icon, new settings folders. The first start moves your OnAir settings and data across.
- Plug-ins use the new names (`saurcom_plugin_entry`, `.saurcomplugin`), so plug-ins built for OnAir must be rebuilt.
- Removed the features that depended on OnAir's servers (shared map, live rooms, update mirror, shared DAB transmitter list, online channel database). Updates come from this repository's GitHub releases only.
