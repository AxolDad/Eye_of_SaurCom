These files are the spoken phrase encoded and decoded where Eye of ZorCom is allowed to encode.

00  original (8 kHz)
01  M17 Codec 2 3200 (vocoder only)
02  M17 Codec 2 1600 (vocoder only)
03  FreeDV 700D
04  FreeDV 700E
05  FreeDV 1600
06  M17 over the air (4FSK -> receiver)

Not in this pack, and they cannot contain this recording:
DMR (4FSK), NXDN 4800/9600, dPMR, P25 Phase 1, D-STAR.
Those vocoders are patented. Eye of ZorCom loads mbelib to decode only; there is no encoder,
so the phrase cannot be turned into AMBE/IMBE bits. Earlier 03/04 files were random
decoder output, not speech — they were removed.
TETRA / TETRAPOL: no speech codec in Eye of ZorCom (signalling only).
FreeDV RADE: UI slot only, not encoded here.
